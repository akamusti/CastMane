_Last updated: October 2026._

## The short version

**CastMane collects no personal data.** There is nothing to opt out of,
because there is nothing being collected.

## The CastMane app

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The app works fully offline.
- **No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.** Nothing leaves your
  computer — not usage statistics, not error reports, not anything else.
- **No network connections.** The app never connects to the internet, so it
  cannot send data anywhere even in theory.
- **Your recordings stay yours.** Video and audio files are saved only on
  your own disk (by default in `~/Videos/CastMane`) and are never uploaded
  or shared by the app.

## This website

- This site is a set of **static pages**. We set no cookies and run no
  analytics or tracking scripts.
- **Search runs entirely in your browser.** Your search queries never leave
  your device.
- The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub itself may keep standard
  server logs when you visit any page it hosts — that is governed by the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement),
  not by us.
